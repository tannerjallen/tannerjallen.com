Win simulator

Blend judges' scores with search buzz (a stand-in for fan votes) and play out the rest of the season.

90% judges' scores 10% search buzz

On the show, judges' scores and viewer votes each count 50%, but Google search is a weak stand-in for votes. On past seasons the model predicted best at about 90% judges, so that's the default.

Model settings

Who wins?

Monte Carlo: thousands of seasons are played out week by week with realistic noise, and the lowest combined score goes home each week. Simple: one weighted strength score per couple, turned into odds. No randomness, nothing played out.
