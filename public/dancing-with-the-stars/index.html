Win simulator

Combine judges' scores with fan votes inferred from eliminations, then play out the rest of the season.

50% judges' scores 50% fan votes

Officially, judges' scores and viewer votes count equally (50%), so that's the default. Results from seasons 30–34 fit about equally well anywhere from 40% to 70%. Each setting re-estimates the fan votes to match.

Model settings

Who wins?

Monte Carlo: plays out thousands of seasons week by week, drawing each couple's fan vote from what the eliminations imply and its judges' scores from its record, with the lowest combined score going home. Simple: one weighted score per couple, turned into odds with nothing played out.
